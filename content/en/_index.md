## About us {#chi-siamo}

![The Il Papavero team](uploads/photos/people.jpeg)

Il Papavero is a non-profit Association for Social Promotion founded by teachers, graduates and professionals working in **school and out-of-school education and training**, who offer study support to help **people of all ages** learn.

We organise cultural activities, workshops, social gatherings and courses for young people, their families and people of all ages, to support their emotional, moral, social, intellectual and civic well-being.
